## Problem

Energy models are only as useful as the loads and parameters behind them, and those are rarely observed
directly. Inferring load profiles and calibrating models against measured time series is therefore central
to trustworthy urban energy modeling.

## Method

- Load profile inference methods that combine machine learning, optimization, and statistical modeling.
- Time-series-based inverse modeling and surrogate-learning workflows for model calibration.

## My Contribution

Doctoral research at Cornell University's Environmental Systems Lab since August 2024, advised by
Prof. Timur Dogan, Prof. Oliver Gao, and Prof. Jacob Mays: developing the inference methods and researching
the inverse-modeling and surrogate-learning workflows described above.
